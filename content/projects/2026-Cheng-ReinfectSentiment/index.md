**Abstract**
The negative effect of respiratory infections (Flu, COVID, etc.) on mental disorders has been widely demonstrated, but few studies link those evidences to repeated infections. Will the psychological trauma from reinfection diminish due to the previous infection experience, or will it accumulate? What roles do the number of infections, the time of infections, the time interval between the previous infection, and the transition between different diseases play in the process? This study collected over 1 million respiratory infections self-reported by 300,000 individuals on social media, together with 7 million users with no self-declaration as candidate controls. We tracked people's expressed sentiment before and after different infection episodes by a quasi-experimental design.
